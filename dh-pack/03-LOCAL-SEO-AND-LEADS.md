## 2. Reviews: the biggest ranking and trust lever
Goal: **15 genuine reviews in 60 days**, then 3-4 per month.

How (all honest, none paid or filtered):
1. Make a short Google review link from the GBP dashboard ("Ask for reviews") and a QR code. Print the QR on handover documents and the studio counter.
2. Send the link by WhatsApp within 2 days of project milestones (plan delivered, permission received, handover).
3. Reply to every review within 48 hours, mention the service and town naturally.
4. Never offer money or discounts for reviews and never only ask happy clients (it breaks Google's rules).

WhatsApp message (English):
> Hello [Name], thank you for trusting DESIGN HAUS with your [house plan / interior / construction]. If you are happy with our work, a short Google review would mean a lot and helps other families in Bankapur and Savanur find us: [link]. If anything could have been better, please tell me directly. - Nadeem

Kannada (needs native review):
> ನಮಸ್ಕಾರ [ಹೆಸರು], DESIGN HAUS ಮೇಲೆ ನಂಬಿಕೆ ಇಟ್ಟಿದ್ದಕ್ಕೆ ಧನ್ಯವಾದಗಳು. ನಮ್ಮ ಕೆಲಸ ಇಷ್ಟವಾಗಿದ್ದರೆ ಈ ಲಿಂಕ್‌ನಲ್ಲಿ ಒಂದು ಸಣ್ಣ Google ವಿಮರ್ಶೆ ಬರೆಯಿರಿ: [link]. ಏನಾದರೂ ಸುಧಾರಿಸಬೇಕಿದ್ದರೆ ನನಗೆ ನೇರವಾಗಿ ತಿಳಿಸಿ. - ನದೀಮ್

Testimonials for the website: ask permission first, use real name + town (or initials), and add a project photo. Do not publish anything the client has not approved.

## 5. Link building (legit, local)
- Ask material suppliers, contractors, interior vendors and structural consultants you work with to link to your site from their pages.
- Offer the HSAQ Masjid committee (and other community projects) a page/mention with the project link (only with their permission).
- Local news/community pages and college/civil-engineering events: share a project story.
- Guest tips on local business pages or Instagram collaborations.
Avoid link buying or directory spam.
