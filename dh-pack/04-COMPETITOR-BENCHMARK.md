## Where Design Haus can win
1. **Hyper-local**: the studio is in Bankapur and covers Savanur, Haveri and nearby. Bigger firms are in Hubli. Own the long tail: "architect in Bankapur", "house plan Savanur", "3D elevation Shiggaon".
2. **Engineer-led, end-to-end**: planning, structural, BOQ, permission and site supervision under one roof. That is what the low-price online sellers cannot offer.
3. **Bilingual (English + Kannada)**: most competitors are English-only. Real Kannada pages are a big local advantage.
4. **Portfolio depth**: 87-90 original designs is large for a studio this size. Show it properly (and make it crawlable).
5. **Sacred/community work**: a masjid project (HSAQ, Bankapur) is a niche. Consider a dedicated "Masjid & community buildings" page, since few local firms showcase this.
6. **Credibility gap to close**: reviews, testimonials, registration number, and real built-project photos (not only 3D) are what the competitors above lack or hide. First to show them wins trust.
