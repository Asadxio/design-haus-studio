CLIENT QUESTIONS & GUIDANCE

Frequently asked questions.

Clear, straightforward answers about our architectural workflow, building approvals, structural engineering, and construction delivery.

What do I need to bring for the first meeting?

+

Your plot dimensions or sale deed copy, a few photos of the site, the direction the plot faces (Vastu orientation), your budget range, and a list of rooms you require.

Do you also help with municipal building permissions?

+

Yes. DESIGN HAUS prepares municipal building permission drawings, sanctions, and assists through the entire approval process across Bankapur, Savanur, and Haveri local bodies.

Is structural engineering design part of the service?

+

Yes. As an engineer-led studio, we engineer the column-beam framework, foundation details, RCC specifications, BOQ material estimates, and provide on-site supervision.

Can you design and build turnkey?

+

Yes. We offer complete turnkey execution from excavation and RCC framing to plastering, flooring, and finishing, as well as design-only consultancies.

Which geographical areas do you serve?

+

We actively serve Bankapur, Savanur, Haveri, Shiggaon, Hangal, Byadgi, and surrounding regions in Karnataka.

Do you work on interior renovations?

+

Yes. We design bespoke spatial layouts, false ceilings, ambient lighting schemes, custom joinery, and complete turnkey interior transformations.

Can we communicate and discuss in Kannada?

+

Yes, absolutely. Our studio team is fluent in both Kannada (ಕನ್ನಡ) and English.

How do we initiate a project?

+

Message us on WhatsApp at +91 90363 89529 with your plot size and site location, or fill out our enquiry form to schedule a site visit.
